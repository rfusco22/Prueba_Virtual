30-Day Limited Parts Warranty

Included with every purchase as our commitment to quality. Covers the cost of parts that break due to normal use within the first 30 days.

Coverage Period: 30 Days from Purchase
What's Covered:
  • Cost of parts that break with normal use within warranty limits
  • Shipping costs for warranty parts
Not Included:
  • Labor or installation costs
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Misuse or improper maintenance
